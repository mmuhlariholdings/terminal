## Context

What problem or question forced a decision. The facts, limits and numbers that matter (say which are hand estimates).

## Decision

## Consequences

What follows from it, good and bad: what else must change, what it rules out, what risks it leaves.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative:** why it was turned down.

## How we check it

The acceptance test, bench test or workflow check that confirms the decision was right (or "not a test: reviewed when ...").
