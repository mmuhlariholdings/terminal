# Code of Conduct

Terminal 1 follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

In short: we want a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Be
respectful and constructive; critique ideas and designs, never people. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

**Reporting.** Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub
(the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form also reaches us privately, or contact a maintainer listed on the
repository). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
