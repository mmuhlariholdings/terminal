## Height exception: CR2032 holder

A CR2032 cell is 3.2 mm thick, so no holder fits the 2.5 mm back-component zone. The Keystone 3034 is about 4-5 mm
(*verify* on the part's drawing). It sits at device X −31 … −11, Y 100 … 124 and uses the 3.2 mm air gap under the carrier
(interfaces.md stack-up), so the antenna flex and any back-cover ribs must stay out of that area. Alternatives if that space is
needed: a CR1220 holder (about 2.5-3 mm, about 40 mAh: still years of backup), or a rechargeable ML414 / supercapacitor fed
from 3.3 V (weeks of backup, not years). Decision for the enclosure owner; noted in interfaces.md 0.2.

## Antenna

The CM4's U.FL socket feeds the flex antenna directly (`dtparam=ant2`); the carrier has no antenna parts. The lead leaves the CM4
and runs up and over the board's top edge to the radio window (silkscreen note on the front). The carrier keeps copper off a
16 × 16 mm area at the CM4's antenna corner (board x 58-74, y 31-47) so the internal antenna still works as a fallback
(`dtparam=ant1`); that corner position was inferred from the IO board's ground cut-out and **must be checked** on the CM4 STEP model.

## Assembly notes

- Order: JLCPCB 4-layer, JLC04161H-7628, impedance control, ENIG, both-side assembly (DF40 / FPC / ESD on the front, the rest on the back).
- Fit the CR2032 cell after assembly (`+` up, away from the board).
- Bring-up: console on TP1 (TX), TP2 (RX), TP3 (GND). Hold TP4 (BOOT) to TP3 at power-up for USB boot. Expected readings in
  `electronics/circuit-notes.md` section 5.
